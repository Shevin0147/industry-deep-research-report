# Source Strategy

## Source Priority

### A-Level Sources: Highest Credibility

Search these first:

- government websites
- regulators
- statistical bureaus
- customs data
- ministries and industry supervisors
- central banks
- EU Commission
- U.S. CBP
- U.S. Census Bureau
- SEC EDGAR
- stock exchange filings
- official policy and standard documents

Use for:

- industry scale
- import/export data
- policy and regulation
- tax, tariff, and compliance requirements
- official statistics

### B-Level Sources: High Credibility

Search next:

- listed-company annual reports
- financial reports
- prospectuses
- investor relations announcements
- Form 10-K / 20-F
- platform official disclosures
- corporate white papers

Use for:

- competitive landscape
- company revenue
- platform business
- user growth
- business model
- cost structure
- company strategy

### C-Level Sources: Medium Credibility

Use after A/B sources:

- brokerage research reports
- consulting company reports
- market database reports
- industry association reports
- professional research institutions
- audit/advisory firm publications

Examples: McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG, Gartner, IDC, Frost & Sullivan, iResearch, QuestMobile, EqualOcean, LeadLeo, CICC, CITIC Securities, Huatai Securities, Guotai Junan Securities, GF Securities.

Use for:

- market forecasts
- trend judgment
- segment estimates
- regional market analysis
- scenario assumptions

### D-Level Sources: Supplementary Only

Use cautiously:

- news reports
- industry media
- blogs
- ordinary webpages
- self-media
- forums and social media
- ecommerce product pages

Use for:

- event supplement
- cases
- user pain points
- product claims
- price bands
- consumer language
- trend signals

D-level sources cannot be the only basis for a core conclusion.

## Independence And Counterevidence

For every core question, search for disconfirming as well as supporting evidence. Trace secondary pages to their original publication and avoid counting syndication, reposts, or multiple reports citing the same dataset as independent confirmation.

After each query group, record what changed, what remains unknown, which competing hypothesis gained or lost support, and whether another search round could change the decision.

## Query Groups

Create several query groups before searching. Use Chinese and English when relevant.

1. Government / regulation / policy:
   - `{industry} 政策 监管 标准`
   - `{industry} site:gov.cn`
   - `{industry} regulation policy standard`

2. Statistical data / market size:
   - `{industry} 市场规模 增速 渗透率`
   - `{industry} market size CAGR penetration`
   - `{industry} customs import export data`

3. Listed company annual report / prospectus:
   - `{key company} 年报 {industry}`
   - `{key company} 招股书 {industry}`
   - `{key company} annual report investor relations`
   - `{industry} prospectus filing`

4. Industry association:
   - `{industry} 协会 白皮书 年鉴`
   - `{industry} association white paper yearbook`

5. Brokerage / consulting / research:
   - `{industry} 行业研究报告`
   - `{industry} 券商 研报 市场空间`
   - `{industry} consulting report market forecast`

6. Competitor / business model:
   - `{industry} 竞争格局 头部企业 市占率`
   - `{company} revenue business model pricing`
   - `{industry} competitive landscape key players`

7. User demand / pain points:
   - `{product} 用户痛点 需求 评论`
   - `{product} reviews complaints use case`
   - `{industry} consumer insight user demand`

8. Trend / risk / opportunity:
   - `{industry} 发展趋势 风险 机会`
   - `{industry} technology trend investment risk`
   - `{industry} financing M&A`

## Citation Requirements

Cite sources for:

- market size
- growth rate
- policy and regulation
- company revenue
- competitive ranking
- user scale
- penetration rate
- industry concentration
- public-company strategy
- major investment or financing information
- core cost or unit-economics assumptions

Do not cite low-quality blogs for key numbers.
